⚡ 2. Katman 1 – Enerji Üretim Hücreleri

Bina, kendi enerjisini çok kaynaklı olarak üretir:

💡 Yenilik:

🌿 Horizon Europe Research Proposal Concept

Project Title: GIBS – Green Intelligent Building System for Net-Positive Energy Urban Ecosystems


---

1. Scientific Vision and Motivation

The building sector is responsible for approximately 36% of global energy consumption and 40% of CO₂ emissions.
Conventional “green building” approaches primarily focus on reducing consumption, not on active energy generation, adaptive intelligence, and systemic interaction.

GIBS introduces a new paradigm:

> Buildings that behave as living systems — generating, storing, sharing, and managing energy intelligently and autonomously.



The project integrates multiphysics energy conversion, AI-based adaptive control, and advanced materials science to develop a self-sustaining, learning, and regenerative building ecosystem.


---

2. Scientific and Technical Objectives

O1. Multi-source Energy Harvesting Integration

Develop hybrid systems combining photovoltaic, piezoelectric, thermoelectric, and electrocaloric layers.

Achieve a net energy production ratio of >1.2 (E_produced / E_consumed) under real building conditions.


O2. AI-based Energy Management and Control

Design an Energy Neural System (ENS) integrating ANFIS and Reinforcement Learning for dynamic energy flow optimization.

Develop predictive control for lighting, HVAC, and power management using real-time data.


O3. Advanced Energy Storage and Thermal Management

Implement solid-state and electrocaloric energy storage units integrated into structural materials.

Demonstrate thermal energy reuse for HVAC systems with >85% efficiency.


O4. Building-to-Building (B2B) Energy Exchange

Create a blockchain-enabled energy trading framework between buildings.

Develop distributed control algorithms ensuring grid stability and fairness in microgrids.


O5. Sustainability, Durability and Lifecycle Impact

Utilize self-healing composites and carbon-absorbing coatings.

Conduct Life Cycle Assessment (LCA) showing >60% CO₂ reduction over conventional buildings.



---

3. Methodology and Work Packages

Work Package	Title	Key Deliverables

WP1	Multi-source Energy Module Design	Prototype PV–piezo–electrocaloric panel
WP2	AI-based Control and Simulation	Digital Twin model for dynamic optimization
WP3	Structural Integration & Materials	Smart wall and flooring systems
WP4	Building-to-Building Energy Trading	Blockchain-based microgrid model
WP5	System Validation and LCA	Demonstration in a real-scale test building
WP6	Dissemination & Exploitation	Policy brief, patents, startup roadmap



---

4. Innovation and Expected Impact

🔬 Scientific Impact

Establish a new interdisciplinary field combining control theory, smart materials, and building physics.

Introduce multi-domain coupling models (electro-thermo-mechanical).


🌍 Environmental Impact

Reduce CO₂ emissions from the building sector by >40%.

Achieve net-positive energy buildings at district level.


💡 Technological Impact

Enable self-adaptive smart surfaces for real-time environmental optimization.

Demonstrate autonomous building control using hybrid ANFIS + Reinforcement Learning models.


💶 Socioeconomic Impact

Contribute to EU Green Deal and Climate-Neutral Cities 2030 missions.

Stimulate new markets for smart materials, energy AI systems, and sustainable construction.



---

5. Consortium and Partners

Potential collaboration fields:

Universities: TU Delft, KU Leuven, EPFL, Politecnico di Milano, METU (for Turkey participation).

Industry Partners: Siemens Smart Infrastructure, Schneider Electric, BASF Materials, or ABB Building Solutions.

Research Centers: European Energy Research Alliance (EERA), Fraunhofer ISE, VTT Finland.



---

6. Expected Outputs (TRL 4→7)

Prototype: Smart energy façade with hybrid conversion and adaptive control.

Simulation platform: MATLAB–Simulink + Python digital twin for multi-domain analysis.

Publications: ≥10 high-impact papers (Energy & Buildings, Applied Energy, IEEE Trans. Industrial Electronics).

Demonstrator: 1 real-scale pilot building in a European city (TRL 7).



---

7. Long-Term Vision

> Transform cities into self-learning ecosystems where every building is an active participant in the energy network.



By merging renewable energy harvesting, intelligent control, and sustainable materials,
GIBS aims to make the built environment a net contributor to the planet’s energy and health balance.
